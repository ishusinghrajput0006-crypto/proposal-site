Ek baat kehni thi...

Tumne kaha tha na,
"Hum tumko reserve nahi karte."

Sach bolun toh mujhe bhi tumhe kisi cheez ke liye force nahi karna. Bas ek baar dil ki baat kehni hai.

🥹

Reservation ki baat nahi hai...

Main tumhe reserve karne ki baat nahi kar raha. Main bas itna keh raha hoon ki main tumhare saath genuinely rehna chahta hoon.

Shayad main perfect nahi hoon, kabhi stupid bhi lag sakta hoon, kabhi zyada effort bhi kar deta hoon... but jo bhi karta hoon, real mein karta hoon.

🌙

Ye koi movie scene nahi...

Main tumhare liye ye sab isliye nahi kar raha kyunki mujhe koi perfect love story banani hai.

Bas tum achhi lagti ho. Tumse baat karna achha lagta hai. Tumhari presence achhi lagti hai.

Aur pata nahi kab, lekin tum mere liye thodi si special ban gayi.

💗

Toh... ek chance dogi?

Main tumse koi promise nahi karwa raha, na hi tumhare upar koi pressure hai.

Bas agar tumhe bhi thoda sa lagta hai ki hum dono ke beech kuch achha ho sakta hai...

toh ek chance mujhe de dena.

Main baaki sab apne efforts se prove kar dunga. ❤️

🫶

Bas itna hi kehna tha...

Tum mujhe reserve karo ya na karo, main tumhe kisi cheez ke liye force nahi karunga.

Bas jo feel karta hoon, woh honestly tumhare saamne rakh diya.

Ab jo bhi tumhara dil kahe, wahi mere liye enough hai. ❤️
